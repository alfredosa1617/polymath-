# Polymath VSN

An elite, multidisciplinary enterprise-grade platform engineered to master complex cognitive domains, financial architecture, gestural linguistics, bio-energetics, and high-density strategic frameworks through interactive simulations.

---

## 🏛️ Project Philosophy

**Polymath:** *A person with exceptional, in-depth knowledge and proficiency across numerous diverse fields, such as art, science, and philosophy.*

Polymath VSN bridges raw technical execution with deep conceptual mastery. Rebuilding complex skill acquisition, the platform eliminates cluttered UI abstractions, superficial diagnostic overlays, and generic workout tracking in favor of high-fidelity, interactive academic environments.

---

## 🎨 Design System

* **Canvas & Cards:** Warm Cream / Beige (`#FDFBF7`, `#FFFCF9`) background with crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) content modules.
* **Borders & Framing:** Fine Warm Taupe (`#EAE3D9`) borders providing subtle spatial separation.
* **Typography & Accents:** Deep Charcoal / Espresso (`#2C221E`) primary text and structural accents.
* **Hero Overview:** A multidisciplinary background collage featuring a central, semi-translucent aesthetic card defining the core Polymath archetype.

---

## 🚀 Flagship Domain Portfolio

### 1. 🔤 Sign Language & Gestural Linguistics
* **Computer Vision Tracking:** Integrates device camera access for real-time 3D hand landmark recognition (MediaPipe / TensorFlow.js).
* **Gesture-to-Text Engine:** Translates dynamic hand motions into active message strings.
* **Interactive Finger-Spelling & Fluency:** Real-time spatial joint validation for static alphabets and dynamic phrase drills.

### 2. 📈 Indian Wealth & Asset Engineering (INR)
* **Compound Growth Engine:** Interactive SIP and Lumpsum accumulation graph visualizers formatted strictly in Indian Rupees (`₹`).
* **Market Cap Allocation Tiering:** Comparative risk-return models for Large Cap (Nifty 50), Mid Cap (Nifty Midcap 150), and Small Cap (Nifty Smallcap 250) funds.
* **ETF vs. Mutual Fund Diagnostics:** Expense ratio analysis, tracking error telemetry, and LTCG/STCG Indian tax efficiency tools.

### 3. 📄 Global Examination Sample Papers
* **International Exam Suite:** Repository covering JLPT (N5, N4, N3, N2, N1), IELTS Academic & General, TOEFL iBT, and Goethe-Zertifikat.
* **Country Metadata Mapping:** Clear visual tagging identifying test origin jurisdictions (Japan, United Kingdom, United States, Germany, France).
* **Interactive Test Runner:** Streamlined, distraction-free examination modules with instant solution validation.

### 4. 🌐 Geopolitics & Strategic Conflicts
* **Territorial & Maritime Sovereignty:** High-density breakdown of active flashpoints (South China Sea, Taiwan Strait, Arctic sea lanes, Kashmir LOC).
* **Verbatim Statement Archives:** Primary source quote comparator contrasting official foreign ministry demarches and bilateral statements.
* **Economic & Resource Security:** Supply chain flow maps, semiconductor sanction analysis, and transboundary water rights tracking.

### 5. ⚡ Bio-Energetics & Longevity Science
* **Mitochondrial Biogenesis Simulator:** Cellular energy adaptation modeling under Zone 2 aerobic training, high-intensity glycolytic stress, and fasting windows.
* **Substrate Crossover Calculator:** Dynamic mapping of lipid (fatty acid) vs. carbohydrate combustion curves.
* **Autonomic Nervous System Balance:** HRV telemetry and sleep architecture stress modeling.

### 6. 🧩 Logic & Spatial Reasoning
* **Interactive Glass Chess Engine:** Grandmaster opening visualization with custom high-contrast obsidian and frosted crystal piece distinction.
* **3D Rubik's Cube & Spatial Drafting:** WebGL-powered 3D orthographic spatial tools.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

* **Backend Framework:** Java 21 / Spring Boot 3
* **Security & Auth:** Spring Security with JWT Authentication
* **3D Graphics & Rendering:** Three.js / WebGL
* **Styling & UI:** Tailwind CSS / Modern CSS Variables
* **Build System:** Apache Maven (`pom.xml`)
